How AI Detection Forensics Works

⚡

2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)

Diffusion models (Stable Diffusion, Midjourney) and GANs use upsampling transposed convolutions and latent VAE decoders. These leave periodic checkerboard artifacts and abnormal high-frequency energy spikes that violate the natural 1/f² power-law of physical camera lenses.

🔍

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Photos from digital cameras show uniform JPEG compression degradation across similar surfaces. AI-rendered images often exhibit stark compression discontinuities between heavily smoothed backgrounds and hyper-detailed features.

🔬

Sensor PRNU & Noise Residuals

Physical cameras possess Photo-Response Non-Uniformity (PRNU) sensor fingerprints and photon shot noise. Generative AI denoises images towards zero Gaussian variance, producing unnatural kurtosis and synthetic micro-gradients.

🏷️

Metadata & EXIF Verification

Scans PNG chunks and EXIF tables for prompt parameters (e.g. CFG scale, sampler, ComfyUI workflows), while cross-checking physical camera lens metadata (shutter speed, ISO, aperture) for authenticity.
